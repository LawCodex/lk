# Prisons (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2005

Prisons (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2005

[Certified on 02nd August, 2005]

L.D.—O. 46/2004.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE P RISONS O RDINANCE

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Prisons (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2005.

#### 2. Amendment of section 79 of the Chapter 54.

2. Section 79 of the Prisons Ordinance (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amended as follows :—

(i) by the repeal of paragraph (g) of that section;

(ii) by the re-lettering of paragraph (h) of that section as paragraph (g).

#### 3. Amendment of section 81 of the principal enactment.

3. Section 81 of the principal enactment is hereby amended as follows :—

(1) in subsection (4) of that section —

(a) by the repeal of paragraph (d) thereof ; and

(b) by the relettering of paragraph (e) thereof as paragraph (d) ;

(2) by the repeal of subsection (5) and (6) of that section ; and

(3) by the renumbering of subsections (7) and (8) as subsections (5) and (6) of that section.

#### 4. Repeal of section 86 of the principal enactment.

4. Section 86 of the principal enactment is hereby repealed.

#### 5. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

5. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
