BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

#### 1. Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Anti-Corruption (Amendment) Act, No. 28 of 2023.

#### 2. Amendment of certain sections of the Act, No. 9 of 2023 specified in the Schedule

2. The sections of the Anti-Corruption Act, No. 9 of 2023 of the Schedule hereto are hereby specified in Column I amended by the substitution for the words specified in the corresponding entry in Column II, of the words specified in the corresponding entry in Column III of that Schedule.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
