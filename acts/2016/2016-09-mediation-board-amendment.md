# Mediation Board (Amendment) Act, No. 9 of 2016

Mediation Board (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 9 of 2016

[Certified on 21st July, 2016]

L. D.—O. 60/2015.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE M EDIATION B OARD A CT , N O . 72 OF 1988

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Mediation Board (Amendment) Act, No. 9 of 2016.

#### 2. Amendment of section 7 of the Act, No. 72 of 1988.

2. Section 7 of the Mediation Board Act, No. 72 of 1988 is hereby amended in paragraph (a) of subsection (1) of that section by the substitution for the words “two hundred and fifty thousand rupees in value;” of the words “five hundred thousand rupees in value;”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
