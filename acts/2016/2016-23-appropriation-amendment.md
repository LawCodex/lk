# Appropriation (Amendment) Act, No. 23 of 2016

1 Appropriation (Amendment) Act, No. 23 of 2016

[Certified on 07th November, 2016]

L.D.—O. 34/2016

A N A CT TO AMEND THE A PPROPRIATION A CT , N O . 16 OF 2015

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

#### 1. title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Short (Amendment) Act, No. 23 of 2016.

#### 2. of 2 of No. 16 of

2. Section 2 of the Appropriation Act, No. 16 of 2015 Amendment section (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby Act, amended by the repeal of paragraph (b) of subsection (1) and 2015. the substitution therefor of the following:—

(b) from the borrowings made in the financial year 2016 which are hereby authorized in terms of relevant laws for moneys to be raised whether in or outside Sri Lanka, for and on behalf of the government: provided that, the balance outstanding of such borrowings at any given time during the financial year 2016 or at the end of the financial year 2016 shall not exceed rupees one thousand six hundred ninety nine billion and the details of such borrowings shall be incorporated in the Final Budget Position Report which is required to be tabled in Parliament under section 13 of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act, No 3 of 2003.
