#### 1. Short title.

1. This act may be cited as the Betting and Gaming Levy (Amendment) Act, No. 14 of 2006.

#### 2. Amendment of Schedule to Act, No. 40 of 1988.

2. The Betting and Gaming Levy Act, No.40 of 1988 as last amended by Act, No.9 of 2005 is hereby further amended in the Schedule to such Act, by the repeal of PART I thereof and the substitution therefor of the following: -
