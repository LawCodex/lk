# Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, No. 49 of 2006

Value Added Tax (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 49 of 2006

[Certified on 15th December, 2006]

L.D.—O. 78/2006.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE V ALUE A DDED T AX A CT , N O . 14 OF 2002

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

#### 1. Short title.

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, No. 49 of 2006.

(2) The provisions of this Act shall for all purposes be deemed to have come into operation on November 17, 2006.

#### 2. Amendment of section 6 of Act, No. 14 of 2002.

2. Section 6 of the Value Added Tax Act, No. 14 of 2002 (as last amended by Act, No. 8 of 2006) is hereby amended by the substitution for the words “seven percentum ; and” of the words, “ten percentum ; and”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the case of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
