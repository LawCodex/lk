# Merchant Shipping (Change of Designation) Act, No. 3 of 2006

Merchant Shipping (Change of Designation) 1 Act, No. 3 of 2006

[Certified on 13th February , 2006]

L.D.—O. 35/2005.

A N A CT TO C HANGE THE D ESIGNATION OF THE D IRECTOR OF M ERCHANT S HIPPING AND TO MAKE SUCH AMENDMENTS IN WRITTEN LAW AS ARE CONSEQUENTIAL .

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Change of Designation) Act, No. 3 of 2006.

#### 2. Amendment to section 3 of the Merchant Shipping Act, No. 52 of 1971.

2. With effect from the date of commencement of this Act, in every context in which “Director of Merchant shipping” appears in—

(a) subsection (1) of section 3 and elsewhere in the Merchant Shipping Act, No. 52 of 1971 and in any other written law; and

(b) any notice, communication, or other document issued, made, required or authorized by or under any written law,

there shall be substituted for the words “Director of Merchant Shipping”, the words “Director-General of Merchant Shipping”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
