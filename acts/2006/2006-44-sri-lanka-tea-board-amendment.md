# Sri Lanka Tea Board (Amendment) Act, No. 44 of 2006

Sri Lanka Tea Board (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 44 of 2006

[Certified on 06th November, 2006]

L.D.—O. 50/2004.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE S RI L ANKA T EA B OARD L AW , N O . 14 OF 1975

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Sri Lanka Tea Board (Amendment) Act, No. 44 of 2006

#### 2. Amendment of section 6 of Law, No. 14 of 1975.

2. Section 6 of the Sri Lanka Tea Board Law, No. 14 of 1975, is hereby amended in paragraph (b) of subsection (1) of section 6 by the repeal of sub-paragraph (iii) of that paragraph and the substitution therefor of the following subparagraphs :—

“(iii) a representative of the Ministry of the Minster in charge of the subject of Plantation Industries, nominated by that Minister ;”,

(iiia) one person nominated by the Minister in charge of the subject of Plantation Industries.”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
