# Samurdhi (Amendment) Act, No. 15 of 2025

Samurdhi (Amendment) Act, No. 15 of 2025

[Certified on 22nd of August, 2025]

L. D. - O 52/2024

A n A ct to amend the S amurdhi A ct , N o . 1 of 2013

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

#### 1. Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Samurdhi (Amendment) Act, No. 15 of 2025.

#### 2. Insertion of new section 28 A in Act, No. 1 of 2013

2. The following new section is hereby inserted immediately after section 28 of the Samurdhi Act, No. 1 of 2013 (in this Act referred to as the “principal enactment”) and shall have effect as section 28 A of that enactment:–

28 A . The provisions of Article 154 of the “Audit of accounts of Constitution relating to audit of accounts of samurdhi public corporations and the provisions of community based the National Audit Act, No. 19 of 2018 shall banks apply to the audit of the accounts of samurdhi community based banks.”.

#### 3. Insertion of new section 35 A in the principal enactment

3. The following new section is hereby inserted immediately after section 35 of the principal enactment and shall have effect as section 35 of that enactment: – A

35 A . The provisions of Article 154 of the “Audit of accounts of Constitution relating to audit of accounts of samurdhi public corporations and the provisions of community based the National Audit Act, No. 19 of 2018 shall banking apply to the audit of the accounts of samurdhi societies community based banking societies.”.

#### 4. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency

4. In the event of any inconsistency between Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
