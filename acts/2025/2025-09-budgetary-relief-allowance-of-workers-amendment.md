# Budgetary Relief Allowance Of Workers (Amendment) Act, No. 9 of 2025

1 Budgetary Relief Allowance of Workers (Amendment) Act, No. 9 of 2025 [Certiﬁ ed on 23rd of July, 2025] L.D.-O 20/2025

A A B R A W A , N . 36 2005

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

#### 1. Short title and date of operation

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Budgetary Relief Allowance of Workers (Amendment) Act, No. 9 of 2025.

(2) The provisions of this Act shall be deemed to have come into operation on April 1, 2025.

#### 2. Amendment of section 3 of Act, No. 36 of 2005

2. Section 3 of the Budgetary Relief Allowance of Workers Act, No. 36 of 2005 is hereby amended in subsection (1) thereof, by the substitution for the words and ﬁ gures "With eﬀ ect from August 1, 2005", of the words and ﬁ gures "For the period commencing on August 1, 2005 and ending on March 31, 2025, ".

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
