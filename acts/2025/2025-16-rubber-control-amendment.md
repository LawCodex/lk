#### 1. title

1. This Act may be cited as the Rubber Control Short (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2025.

#### 2. to 436

2. (1) The Rubber Control Act (Chapter 436) (hereinafter General Amendment referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amended Chapter by the substitution for the words, “Controller” and “Rubber Controller” wherever such words appear in the principal enactment of the words, “Director-General”.

(2) Every reference to the “Controller” and “Rubber Controller” in any other written law or in any notice, notification, contract, communication, form, other instrument or document shall from and after the date of commencement of this Act be read and construed as a reference made to the “Director-General of the Rubber Development Department”.
