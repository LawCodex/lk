#### 1. Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Intellectual Property (Amendment) Act, No. 8 of 2022.

#### 2. Amendment of section 2 of Act, No.36 of 2003

2. Section 2 of the Intellectual Property Act, No.36 of 2003 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amended in paragraph (a) of subsection (2), by the substitution for the words “administration of Industrial Designs, Patents, Marks and of any other matter” of the words “administration of Industrial Designs, Patents, Marks, Geographical Indications and of any other matter”.

#### 3. Amendment of section 4 of the principal enactment

3. Section 4 of the principal enactment is hereby amended as follows:-

(1) in subsection (1), by the substitution for the words “Industrial designs, patents, marks and any other matter” of the words “Industrial designs, patents, marks, geographical indications and any other matter”;

(2) in subsection (2), by the substitution for the words “industrial designs, patents, marks and any other matter” of the words “industrial designs, patents, marks, geographical indications and any other matter”;

#### 4. Amendment of section 101 of the principal enactment

4. Section 101 of the principal enactment is hereby amended by the repeal of the definition of the phrase “geographical indication”.
