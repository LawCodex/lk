# Fauna and Flora Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 7 of 2022

Fauna and Flora Protection (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 7 of 2022

[Certified on 02nd of March, 2022]

L. D.— O. 13/2017

A N A CT TO AMEND THE F AUNA AND F LORA P ROTECTION O RDINANCE (C HAPTER 469)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

#### 1. Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Fauna and Flora Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 7 of 2022.

#### 2. Replacement of the long title to Chapter 469

2. The long title to the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance (Chapter 469) (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby repealed and the following long title substituted therefor:—

“AN ORDINANCE TO PROVIDE FOR THE PROTECTION AND CONSERVATION OF THE FAUNA AND FLORA OF SRI LANKA AND THEIR HABITATS; FOR THE PREVENTION OF COMMERCIAL AND OTHER MISUSE OF SUCH FAUNA AND FLORA AND THEIR HABITATS; FOR THE CONSERVATION OF BIODIVERSITY OF SRI LANKA; TO TAKE SUCH MEASURES TO GIVE EFFECT TO THE CONVENTION ON INTERNATIONAL TRADE IN ENDANGERED SPECIES OF WILD FAUNA AND FLORA (CITES); AND TO PROVIDE FOR MATTERS CONNECTED THEREWITH OR INCIDENTAL THERETO.”.

#### 3. Amendment of section 71 of the principal enactment

3. Section 71 of the principal enactment is hereby amended in subsection (2) thereof, by the insertion immediately after paragraph (f) of that subsection, of the following new paragraphs:–

“(fa) all matters necessary for the enforcement of the provisions of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES), subject to the prior written approved of the Cabinet of Ministers;

#### 4. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency

4. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
