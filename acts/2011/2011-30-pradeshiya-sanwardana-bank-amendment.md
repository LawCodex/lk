# Pradeshiya Sanwardana Bank (Amendment) Act, No. 30 of 2011

Pradeshiya Sanwardana Bank (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 30 of 2011

[Certified on 07th June, 2011]

L.D.—O 13/2011.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE P RADESHIYA S ANWARDANA B ANK A CT , N O . 41 OF 2008

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:-

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Pradeshiya Sanwardana Bank (Amendment) Act, No. 30 of 2011.

#### 2. Replacement of section 4 of Act, No. 41 of 2008.

2. Section 4 of the Pradeshiya Sanwardana Bank Act, No. 41 of 2008 is hereby repealed and the following Section substituted therefor :—

#### 4. of

4. The head office of the Bank may be “Head office the Bank. established in any location in Sri Lanka, outside the Administrative District of Colombo, as may be determined by the Board.”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
