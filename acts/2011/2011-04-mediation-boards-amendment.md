# Mediation Boards (Amendment) Act, No. 4 of 2011

Mediation Boards (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 4 of 2011

[Certified on 23rd February, 2011]

L.D.—O. 25/2010.

A N A CT TO A MEND THE M EDIATION B OARDS A CT , N O . 72 OF 1988

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Mediation Boards (Amendment) Act, No. 4 of 2011.

#### 2. Amendment of section 7 of Act, No. 72 of 1988.

2. Section 7 of the Mediation Boards Act, No. 72 of 1988 is hereby amended in subsection (1) of that section as follows:—

(1) by the substitution in paragraph (a) of that subsection for the words “twenty five thousand rupees in value” of the words “two hundred and fifty thousand rupees in value;”;

(2) by the substitution in paragraph (c) of that subsection for the words “Second Schedule to this Act,” of the words “Second Schedule to this Act; or”; and

(3) by the insertion immediately after paragraph (c) of that subsection, of the following paragraph:—

“(d) the offence under section 367 or 368 B of the Penal Code is committed by a person below the age of eighteen years, in respect of any property, the value of which does not exceed rupees five thousand,”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
