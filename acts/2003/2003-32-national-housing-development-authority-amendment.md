# National Housing Development Authority (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 2003

National Housing Development Authority (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 2003

[Certified on 22nd October, 2003]

L.D.–O. 36/2003.

*A N CT*

A TO AMEND THE N ATIONAL H OUSING D EVELOPMENT A UTHORITY A CT , N O . 17 OF 1979

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the National Housing Development Authority (Amendment) Act, No. 32 of 2003.

#### 2. Repeal of section 10 of Act No. 17 of 1979.

2. Section 10 of the National Housing Development Authority Act, No. 17 of 1979 is hereby repealed.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
