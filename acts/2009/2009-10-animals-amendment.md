#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Animals (Amendment) Act, No. 10 of 2009.

#### 2. Insertion of new section 3 AA in Act No.29 of 1958.

2. The Animals Act, No. 29 of 1958 (hereinafter referred to as the principal enactment) is hereby amended by the insertion immediately after section 3 thereof of the A following new section which shall have effect as section 3 AA of that enactment :—

3 AA . (1) Notwithstanding anything to the “Care of animals contrary in any other written law, where an against offence in relation to an animal is committed whom and where such offence is brought to the notice offences are of the Magistrate having competent committed. jurisdiction, the Magistrate may, pending the determination of the case, make an interim Order to the effect that the animal against whom the offence has been committed—

(a) be handed into the custody of an Animal Care Centre approved by the Minister by Order published in the Gazette;

(b) be handed over to any non-governmental organization whose primary objective is concerned with the welfare of animals; or

(c) be handed over to the custody of any person whom the court is satisfied, is actively engaged in caring for animals.

#### 3. Renumbering of section 3 D of the principal enactment.

3. Section 3 D of the principal enactment is hereby amended by the renumbering of that section as section 3E of that enactment.

#### 4. Insertion of new section 3 D in the principal enactment.

4. The following new section is hereby inserted immediately before the section 3 of the principal enactment E and shall have effect as section 3 D of that enactment:—

“Penalty for 3 D . Any person who contravenes the the offences provisions of this Part of this Act or any committed regulation made thereunder, shall commit an under Part II. offence and shall on conviction after summary trail by a Magistrate be liable to a fine not exceeding fifty thousand rupees or to imprisonment of either description for a term not exceeding three years or to both such fine and imprisonment.”.

#### 5. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

5. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
