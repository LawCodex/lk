#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Fisheries (Regulation of Foreign Fishing Boats) (Amendment) Act, No. 1 of 2018.

#### 2. General Amendment to Act, No. 59 of 1979.

2. The Fisheries (Regulation of Foreign Fishing Boats)Act, No. 59 of 1979 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amended by the substitution for the word “Secretary”, wherever that word occurs in the principal enactment or any regulation, rule, notice or notification of the word “Director-General”.
