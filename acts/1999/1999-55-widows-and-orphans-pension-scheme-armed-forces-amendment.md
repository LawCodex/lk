# Widows ' and Orphans' Pension Scheme (Armed Forces) (Amendment) Act, No. 55 of 1999

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :-

#### 1. Short Title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Widows and Orphans Pension Scheme (Armed Forces) (Amendment) Act, No.55 of 1999.

#### 2. Insertion of new section 3 B in Act, No.18 of 1970.

2. The following new section is hereby inserted immediately after section 3 and shall have effect as section A 3 B of the Widows and Orphans Pension Scheme (Armed Forces) Act, No. 18 of 1970 :-

3 B . (1) Any Person who became a member Extension of time limit to of the Armed Forces on or before September become a 30th 1968 and who - contributor to the Pension (a) continued, or continues, to be such Scheme member after that date ; and

(b) has not elected, under section 3 or section 3 A , to be a contributor to the Pension Scheme,

may, before such date as the Minister may appoint by Order published in the Gazette, elect to be a contributor to the Pension Scheme :

Provided however, that where a member of the armed forces referred to in this subsection has died prior to the coming into force of this section or dies before the date appointed under this subsection without making an election H-22545
