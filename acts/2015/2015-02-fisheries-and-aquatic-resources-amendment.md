BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (Amendment) Act, No. 2 of 2015.

#### 2. Amendment of section 49 of Act, No. 2 of 1996.

2. Section 49 of the Fisherise and Aquatic Resources Act, No. 2 of 1996 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amended by the repeal of subsection (2 A ) of that section and the substitution therefor of the following subsection:—

“(2 A ) Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions of sections 14 A or 14 F of this Act shall commit an offence and shall on conviction after summary trial before a Magistrate be liable to an imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to a fine not less than the amounts specified in Column II of the Schedule hereto which shall be determined by taking into consideration the length of the fishing boat specified in corresponding entry in the Column I of the Schedule:—
