# Immigrants and Emigrants (Amendment) Act, No. 7 of 2015

1 Immigrants and Emigrants (Amendment) Act, No. 7 of 2015

[Certified on 03rd June, 2015]

L. D.—O. 25/2013.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE I MMIGRANTS AND E MIGRANTS A CT (C HAPTER 351)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Immigrants and Emigrants (Amendment) Act, No. 7 of 2015.

#### 2. Insertion of new section 35 A in Chapter 351.

2. The Immigrants and Emigrants Act (Chapter 351) is hereby amended by the insertion, immediately after section 35 thereof, of the following new section:—

35 A . The Authority prescribed to issue or “Taking of finger renew passports may take finger printing for printing for the inclusion in the passport, from a person passport applying for a passport or the renewal of a purposes. passport.”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
