# Licensing Of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non-Vessel Operatingcommon Carriers And Container Act, No. 4 of 2026

[Certified on 17th of February, 2026]

L.D.-O 62/2023 A n A ct to amend the L icensing of S hipping A gents , F reight F orwarders , N on -V essel O perating C ommon C arriers and C ontainer O perators A ct , N o . 10 of 1972; and to provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto .

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows: -

#### 1. Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Licensing of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non-Vessel Operating Common Carriers and Container Operators (Amendment) Act, No. 4 of 2026.

#### 2. Amendment of long title to Act, No.10 of 1972

2. The long title to the Licensing of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non-Vessel Operating Common Carriers and Container Operators Act, No. 10 of 1972, (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amended by the substitution for the words “F reight F orwarders , N on -V essel O perating C ommon C arriers , and C ontainer O perators ”, of the words “F reight F orwarders and N on -V essel O perating C ommon C arriers ”.

#### 3. Licensing of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non -Vessel Operating Common Carriers, and Container Operators Act, and the Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non -Vessel Operating Common Carriers, and Container Operators to be known as the Licensing of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders and Non-Vessel Operating Common Carriers Act, and the Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders and Non-Vessel Operating Common Carriers respectively

3. (1) In the principal enactment and in any other written law, there shall be substituted –

(a) for the words, “Licensing of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non - Vessel Operating Common Carriers and Container Operators Act”, of the words “Licensing of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders and Non-Vessel Operating Common Carriers Act”; and

(b) for the words, “Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non - Vessel Operating Common Carriers, and Container Operators”, of the words “Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders and Non-Vessel Operating Common Carriers”.

(2) Every reference to the “Licensing of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders, Non - Vessel Operating Common Carriers and Container Operators Act” in any notice, notification, contract, communication or other document shall be read and construed as a reference to the “Licensing of Shipping Agents, Freight Forwarders and Non-Vessel Operating Common Carriers Act”.

#### 4. Amendment of section 15 of the principal enactment

4. Section 15 of the principal enactment is hereby amended by the deletion of the definition of expression “container operators”.

#### 5. Savings and transitional provisions

5. With effect from the date of commencement of this Act, notwithstanding any provision in any other law-

(a) any application made for the issue of licence in respect of container operator under subsection (1 A ) of section 3 of the principal enactment and pending on the day immediately preceding the date of commencement of this Act shall be deemed to be made under the provisions of the Licensing of Container Depot Operators Act, No. 3 of 2026; and

(b) unless specifically suspended or cancelled in the manner provided for in the principal enactment, all licences issued to container operator, and subsisting or having effect on the day immediately preceding the date of commencement of this Act shall be deemed with effect from the date of commencement of this Act to be licences issued under the provisions of the Licensing of Container Depot Operators Act, No. 3 of 2026.

*6. Sinhala text to prevail in case of and inconsistency*

In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
