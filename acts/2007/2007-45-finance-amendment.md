# Finance (Amendment) Act, No. 45 of 2007

Finance (Amendment ) Act, No. 45 of 2007 1

[Certified on 15th October, 2007]

L. D.—O. 39/2007.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE F INANCE A CT , N O . 5 OF 2005

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Finance (Amendment) Act, No. 45 of 2007

#### 2. Amendment of the First Schedule of the Finance Act, No. 5 of 2005.

2. The First Schedule of the Finance Act, No. 5 of 2005 is hereby amended by the addition, at the end of that Schedule of the following item:—

“5. The Inland Revenue Act, No. 10 of 2006 ( other than the provisions of Chapter XVI, XVII, XXI and sections 36 and 65). ”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
