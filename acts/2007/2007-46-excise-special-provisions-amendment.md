# Excise (Special Provisions) (Amendment) Act, No. 46 of 2007

Excise (Special Provisions) (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 46 of 2007

[Certified on 15th October, 2007]

L.D. — O. 36 /2007

A N A CT TO AMEND THE E XCISE (S PECIAL P ROVISIONS ) A CT , N O . 13 OF 1989

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :–

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Excise (Special Provisions) (Amendment) Act, No. 46 of 2007.

#### 2. Amendment of section 5 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989.

2. Section 5 of the Excise (Special Provisions) Act, No. 13 of 1989 is hereby amended, in sub-paragraph (ii) of paragraph (b) of subsection (2) of that section by the substitution for the words “at the rate of ten per centum” of the words “at the rate of fifteen per centum”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of any inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
