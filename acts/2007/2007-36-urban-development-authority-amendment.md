# Urban Development Authority (Amendment) Act, No. 36 of 2007

Urban Development Authority (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 36 of 2007

[Certified on 18th September, 2007]

L.D.—O. 18/2007.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE U RBAN D EVELOPMENT A UTHORITY L AW , N O . 41 OF 1978

BE it therefore enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :—

#### 1. Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Urban Development Authority (Amendment) Act, No. 36 of 2007.

#### 2. Amendment of section 8 of Law No. 41 of 1978.

2. Section 8 of the Urban Development Authority Law, No. 41 of 1978 (hereinafter referred to as the “principal enactment”) is hereby amended by the insertion immediately after paragraph (c) of that section, of the following paragraph :—

“(cc) to formulate a Scheme for rainwater harvesting to be included in the Development Plan prepared in terms of section 8 A which scheme shall be in keeping with the National Rainwater Policy and Strategies ;”;

#### 3. Amendment of Schedule of the Principal enactment.

3. The Schedule to the principal enactment is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (e) of item 4 thereof, of the following paragraph:—

“(e) drainage of site and roof including measures necessary for rainwater harvesting;”.

#### 4. Sinhala text to Prevail in case of inconsistency.

4. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
