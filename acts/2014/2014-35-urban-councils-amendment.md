# Urban Councils (Amendment) Act, No. 35 of 2014

Urban Councils (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 35 of 2014

[Certified on 23rd October, 2014]

L.D.—O. 03/2014.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE U RBAN C OUNCILS O RDINANCE (C HAPTER 255)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

#### 1. Short title and date of operation.

1. This Act may be cited as the Urban Councils (Amendment) Act, No. 35 of 2014 and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may appoint by Order Published in the Gazette.

#### 2. Insertion of new section 178 B in the Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255).

2. The following new section is hereby inserted immediately after section 178 of the Urban Councils A Ordinance (Chapter 255) and shall have effect as section 178 B of that Ordinance :—

178 B . The provisions of the proviso to “Transitional provision. section 178 of the Urban Councils Ordinance A as amended by the Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 21 of 2012 shall not be applicable to any budget or supplementary budget to be submitted to an Urban Council constituted in terms of elections held prior to the date of coming into operation of Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 21 of 2012 and Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012 respectively.”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
