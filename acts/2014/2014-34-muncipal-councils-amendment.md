# Muncipal Councils (Amendment) Act, No. 34 of 2014

Municipal Councils (Amendment) 1 Act, No. 34 of 2014

[Certified on 23rd October, 2014]

L.D.—O. 03/2014.

A N A CT TO AMEND THE M UNICIPAL C OUNCILS O RDINANCE (C HAPTER 252)

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:—

#### 1. Short title and date of operation.

1. This Act may be cited as the Municipal Councils (Amendment) Act, No. 34 of 2014 and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may appoint by Order Published in the Gazette.

#### 2. Insertion of new section 215 B in the Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252).

2. The following new section is hereby inserted immediately after section 215 of the Municipal Councils A Ordinance (Chapter 252) and shall have effect as section 215 B of that Ordinance :—

“Transitional 215 . The provisions of the proviso to B provision. section 215 A of the Municipal Councils Ordinance as amended by the Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 21 of 2012 shall not be applicable to any budget or supplementary budget to be submitted to a Municipal Council constituted in terms of the results of elections held prior to the date of coming into operation of Local Authorities (Special Provisions) Act, No. 21 of 2012 and Local Authorities Elections (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2012 respectively.”.

#### 3. Sinhala text to prevail in case of inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail.
